# Lessons for KOML

The domain needs evidence with explicit timestamps and narrowly defined rules. A failed contact endpoint and a missed public deadline support different conversations; combining them into an invented opportunity score would hide what was observed.

The synthetic experiment validates that the rules find all 90 planted cases among 200 observations without false positives, and that changing an observation changes the output. It does not validate the assumption that these signals predict paid work. That assumption remains untested until Henry records real use and hand-checks the results.

Operating rule: define the observation schema, independent oracle, and rejection boundaries before adding ranking or automation. Preserve author-declared provenance in every report and measure real accuracy before expanding scope.
